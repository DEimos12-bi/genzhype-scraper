THE SCRIPT
A popular naval war game just admitted to copying an artist's vehicle design. And they are selling it to players for up to seven hundred dollars.
The game is Modern Warships. The creator is concept artist Alex Jay Brady, who drew the original flying ship back in twenty-fourteen.
Brady spotted the ship in-game and posted the proof on X. The developers copied everything, including non-functional design mistakes like planes taking off backward.
IGN reports the vehicle is locked behind gacha spins costing up to one hundred dollars, or seven hundred dollars to buy outright.
Brady says they did not give permission, but wrote that if the studio had just sent some money, they would have zero issues.
Then the developers did something rare. They publicly replied, apologized, and admitted they used the design as a reference without proper clearance.
Kotaku reports the studio is not removing the vehicle. Instead, they are trying to reach a deal to pay the artist directly.
So what should happen? Should the studio pay the artist to keep it, or remove the design completely? Comment one word. Pay, or remove.
