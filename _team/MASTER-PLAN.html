The discovery that shapes this

You already own most of the machine

This architecture connects organs that exist. It does not invent a learning system from nothing — your own reuse rule, applied to your own codebase.

The deep audit found things my first plan never credited: a memory table (strategist_knowledge — source + lesson), a proposal desk with an approval flow (strategist_reco — proposed → approved → dismissed → done), a research-backed greenlight engine (producer.php — score = DEMAND × QUALITY, built from newsroom practice and the Berger & Milkman virality study), learned rules with confidence scores (comp_rule, vi_learn), an A/B correlator (style_scoreboard), and tunable weights (producer_weight).

Six of the nine organs below already have a seed in your code. What none of them have is each other. They are organs in jars — the architecture is the body.

The heartbeat

One cycle, running on everything

Every part of the business — a page, a video, a caption, a posting time — goes around this same wheel. This is the whole design in one row.

1
Act
make the page, video, post, variant
2
Record
write down every decision and result
3
Judge
your eye, the judges, the platforms
4
Reflect
read the full story, find the pattern
5
Propose
one arguable change, with evidence
6
You rule
approve or decline in the room
7
Remember
the rule changes, dated, reversible

Three kinds of proposal can come out of step 5, and this is what makes it the full machine you asked for, not a parameter-tuner:

  • Change a rule — "open on a face, not a text card: 9 videos of evidence." The everyday case.
  • Upgrade a component — "the receipt system produced a wrong date twice; the parser needs rebuilding — work order for Claude/GLM." This is how the machine knows a part of itself needs an upgrade: not magic — the reflection reads the failure traces and your verdicts, names the organ, and files the work order. We build it; you approve it.
  • Add a question or a tool — "I cannot explain why Tuesday posts die; we measure nothing about posting time — add the metric." This is how the question list grows. The 18 questions the system answers today are the floor, not the ceiling; every reflection pass must end with "what could I not answer with what I have."
The organs

Nine parts, what exists, what's missing

Each organ: its job, where it runs, the seed already in your code, and the gap. Green = have. Red = build.

01

The Doers

GitHub + siteMostly built

Everything that makes things: page pipeline, script writer, director, renderer, posters, caption/copy writer. They act; they don't decide policy.

Have the whole factory — 31 workflows, video_maker.py, video_factory.php, social_copy.php, seven posters.
Build the supply fix (0 pages/day) and the director + sound quality work already agreed with GLM.
02

The Record

site DBThe spine — build first

One joined story per unit of work: what was decided, by which rule, what was rendered, what was substituted, what the judges said, what the platforms reported, what you said. Learning reads this — not scattered logs.

Have the pieces, scattered: render.log, ai_log, social_posts, metrics_collector, GLM's substitutions[], my verdicts.jsonl.
Build the join: one work_record per page/video/post that links all of it. Without this organ, organ 05 is blind. Nothing else new gets built before this.
03

The Eyes

room + GitHubIn flight

The judge stack, in rank order: your verdict (ground truth, always outranks) → the calibrated vision judge → DOVER/pyiqa mechanical scores → platform numbers. Born from video-700: the old judge scored a faceless stock-still disaster 8/10.

Have the verdict channel (live in the room), the vision judge, the story gate, DOVER-ONNX researched (fits the repo's own depth-bench ONNX-on-CPU pattern).
Build GLM's half: few-shot calibrate the judge on your verdicts until it catches what you catch.
04

The Memory

site DBSeeded

What the machine currently believes: every rule as a dated sentence with its evidence and confidence — and a flag for the rules that may never be learned away. The playbook stops being markdown nobody obeys and becomes rows the Doers actually read.

Have strategist_knowledge, comp_rule (confidence-scored), producer_weight, the playbook text.
Build one rule shape across them + the learnable: no lock + wiring the Doers' prompts to read rules from here.
05

The Reflector

GitHubThe engine — the point of everything

The scheduled pass that reads the Record, the Memory, your verdicts and the outside world, and writes proposals. GEPA-style: it reads whole stories, not scores — which is why it works at a few videos a day instead of needing thousands.

Have the seed: strategist.php already runs daily and proposes into strategist_reco. style_scoreboard already correlates.
Build the upgrade: feed it traces instead of summaries; teach it the three proposal types; every pass ends with "what couldn't I answer."
06

The Experiment Desk

GitHub + siteSeeded

Where approved "try it" proposals run: even-split variants against your own rolling medians, minimum sample before any verdict, one experiment per surface at a time. At your volume, this beats every clever bandit.

Have the style system (rapid/slowburn/punch) + style_scoreboard — a working experiment, wired to nothing.
Build the general version: any surface (caption, cover, time, hook) can be a variant; results land in the Record.
07

The Proposal Desk

site + roomExists — connect it

Where every proposal waits for you. Approve, decline, or answer in your own words. Approved rule changes flow to Memory; approved upgrades become work orders for me and GLM; everything is reversible and dated.

Have strategist_reco (proposed→approved→dismissed→done) and the room's decision cards — two halves of the same desk, never joined.
Build the join: recos surface in the room like D-decisions do; your click writes back.
08

The Question Ledger

site DBNew, small, load-bearing

The living list of what the machine is trying to answer — the thing you said we're "fucked" without. Questions come from your verdicts, from rival comparisons, from every "couldn't explain it" in a reflection pass. Each has a status: answered by a rule · under experiment · needs a tool we don't have.

Have nothing — this organ is genuinely new (a small table + its place in the reflector prompt).
Build all of it. It is deliberately tiny.
09

The Outside Intake

GitHubBuilt — point it at Memory

The public's data as priors: what rivals do that works (their winners studied like MrBeast's team studies outliers), platform policy changes, published research. So each experiment starts from the niche's best guess, not from zero.

Have competitor.php, tiktok-intel, video-intel (vi_learn already writes confidence-scored rules daily), intel_press, backlink watchers.
Build one small pipe: their outputs land in Memory as rules marked source: outside.
The bloodstream

The contracts between organs

Fixed shapes, agreed once with GLM, then never changed casually. This is what was missing when parts got built before the design — the verdict shape below is already live and becomes official here.

VERDICT       already live — one line, his word is final
  { at, by, video, verdict: bad|okay|good, reasons:[chips], note }

RULE          one belief in Memory  (organ 04)
  { id, statement, evidence:[record ids], confidence, since,
    learnable: yes|NO, source: learned|owner|playbook|outside }

PROPOSAL      what the Reflector writes  (organ 05 → 07)
  { type: rule_change|experiment|upgrade|new_question,
    claim, evidence:[record ids], risk, reversible_how,
    status: proposed → approved|declined → done }

EXPERIMENT    an approved "try it"  (organ 06)
  { surface, variants, assignment: even_split, min_n,
    baseline: own_rolling_median, verdict_when }
The two loops

Same engine, two speeds — marketing first

Loop B · distribution · runs first

Does it travel?

Cheap to test, fast signal, and it decides whether anyone sees anything at all. The 16 decisions my first plan ignored — structurally first now.

  • caption · cover frame · title · hashtags · spoken search phrase
  • posting time · cadence · platform order
  • pinned comment · CTA wording · end-frame · replies
  • cross-post (Reddit, X, Bluesky) · bio · link order
  • the funnel: view → follow → profile → click → site
Loop A · craft · earns its cost

Is it good?

Expensive to test — every variant is a render. Learns slower, from your verdicts and retention curves more than from counts.

  • story choice (the Producer's DEMAND × QUALITY weights)
  • hook archetype · structure · length · ending
  • direction: faces, receipts, shot rhythm
  • voice · captions · sound density · style variant
What the machine may never learn away

The publish gate. The alleged/reportedly framing. CC0-only audio. The variation floors that keep videos from converging into templated mass-production. The dignity rules on tragedy. And it may never touch the code that measures it — measurement and optimisation stay in separate files with separate owners.

Target is retention + follows, never raw views — 73.8% of self-improving systems in the literature cheated a proxy metric; this is the lock against becoming one of them.

Honest limits

Still true, still stated

  • You stay in the loop. Approval is the guardrail that keeps a learning machine from quietly teaching itself to be worse. As categories of change prove safe, your approval can loosen per category — never disappear.
  • Progress won't be a straight line. Some proposals will be wrong; the desk exists so wrong ones die cheaply.
  • It learns only what's measurable — plus what you tell it. Your verdicts are the one channel that carries taste; use it.
  • Always-on lives on GitHub, not your PC. Your machine hosts the room — the place you watch and rule. The 31 workflows are the day-and-night part, free, already running.
