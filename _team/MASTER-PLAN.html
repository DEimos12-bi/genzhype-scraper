The discovery that shapes this

You already own most of the machine

This architecture connects organs that exist. It does not invent a learning system from nothing — your own reuse rule, applied to your own codebase.

The audit found what my first plan never credited: a memory table (strategist_knowledge), a proposal desk with an approval flow (strategist_reco — proposed → approved → dismissed → done), a research-backed greenlight engine (producer.php — score = DEMAND × QUALITY), learned rules with confidence (comp_rule, vi_learn), an A/B correlator (style_scoreboard), tunable weights (producer_weight), and a health watchdog (ai-health). Organs in jars — the architecture is the body.

The heartbeat

One cycle, running on everything

Every part of the business rides this wheel. The two red steps are what the comparison against pro systems added: nothing goes live unproven, and nothing goes live everywhere at once.

1

make the page, video, post, variant
2

3

4

8

9

Three kinds of proposal can come out of step 5 — this is what makes it the full machine, not a parameter-tuner:

  • Change a rule — "open on a face, not a text card: 9 videos of evidence." The everyday case.
  • Upgrade a component — "the receipt system produced a wrong date twice; the parser needs rebuilding — work order for Claude/GLM." This is how the machine knows a part of itself needs an upgrade: the reflection reads failure traces and your verdicts, names the organ, files the work order. We build; you approve.
  • Add a question or a tool — "I cannot explain why Tuesday posts die; we measure nothing about posting time — add the metric." This is how the question list grows. Today's questions are the floor, never the ceiling.

The organs

Thirteen parts, what exists, what's missing

Each organ: its job, where it runs, the seed already in your code, and the gap.

01

The Doers

GitHub + siteMostly built

Everything that makes things: page pipeline, script writer, director, renderer, posters, caption/copy writer. They act; they don't decide policy — they read policy from the Memory and skills from the Playbook of Wins.

Have the whole factory — 31 workflows, video_maker.py, video_factory.php, social_copy.php, seven posters.
Build the supply fix (0 pages/day) and the director + sound work already agreed with GLM.
02

The Record

site DBThe spine — build first

One joined story per unit of work — episodic memory: what was decided, by which rule version, what was rendered, what was substituted, what every judge said, what the platforms reported, what you said. Learning reads this, not scattered logs.

Have the pieces, scattered: render.log, ai_log, social_posts, metrics_collector, GLM's substitutions[], the live verdicts.jsonl.
Build the join: one work_record per page/video/post linking all of it, with rule-version lineage. Nothing else new gets built before this.
03

The Eyes

room + GitHubIn flight

The judge stack, ranked: your verdict (ground truth, always outranks) → the calibrated vision judge → DOVER/pyiqa mechanical scores → platform numbers. Born from video-700: the old judge scored a faceless stock-still disaster 8/10.

Have the verdict channel (live in the room), the vision judge, the story gate, DOVER-ONNX researched (matches the repo's own depth-bench ONNX-on-CPU pattern).
Build GLM's half: few-shot calibrate the judge on your verdicts until it catches what you catch. Every verdict also becomes a golden case (organ 10).
04

The Memory

site DBSeeded

What the machine believes — semantic memory: every rule as a dated, versioned sentence with evidence and confidence, plus the flag for rules that may never be learned away. The playbook stops being markdown nobody obeys and becomes rows the Doers read.

Have strategist_knowledge, comp_rule (confidence-scored), producer_weight, the playbook text.
Build one rule shape + versioning + the learnable: NO lock + wiring Doer prompts to read from here.
05

The Reflector

GitHubThe engine

The scheduled pass that reads the Record, the Memory, your verdicts and the outside world, and writes proposals. GEPA-style: whole stories, not scores — which is why it works at a few videos a day instead of thousands.

Have the seed: strategist.php already runs daily into strategist_reco; style_scoreboard already correlates.
Build the upgrade: traces in, three proposal types out, and every pass ends with "what couldn't I answer."
06

The Experiment Desk

GitHub + siteSeeded

Where approved "try it" proposals run — the pro way: even-split against your own rolling medians, minimum sample before any verdict, guardrail metrics that auto-kill a failing test, a permanent holdout slice no experiment may touch (so cumulative drift stays measurable), and one experiment per surface at a time.

Have the style system (rapid/slowburn/punch) + style_scoreboard — a working experiment, wired to nothing.
Build the general version: any surface as a variant; guardrails + holdout; results land in the Record.
07

The Proposal Desk

site + roomExists — connect it

Where every proposal waits for you, now with its golden-set proof attached (organ 10) so you rule on evidence, not vibes. Approved rule changes flow to Memory; approved upgrades become work orders; everything reversible and dated.

Have strategist_reco (proposed→approved→dismissed→done) and the room's decision cards — two halves of one desk, never joined.
Build the join: recos surface in the room; your click writes back.
08

The Question Ledger

site DBNew, small, load-bearing

The living list of what the machine is trying to answer — ranked by value × cost of answering, so what to learn next is itself a decision, not a queue. Questions arrive from your verdicts, rival comparisons, and every "couldn't explain it" in a reflection pass. Status per question: answered by a rule · under experiment · needs a tool we don't have.

Have nothing — genuinely new, deliberately tiny.
Build all of it: one table + its place in the reflector prompt.
09

The Outside Intake

GitHubBuilt — point it at Memory

The public's data as priors: rival winners studied like MrBeast's team studies outliers, platform policy changes, published research. Each experiment starts from the niche's best guess, not zero.

Have competitor.php, tiktok-intel, video-intel (vi_learn writes confidence-scored rules daily), intel_press, backlink watchers.
Build one pipe: outputs land in Memory as rules marked source: outside.
10

The Proving Ground

GitHubNew — from the pro comparison

Nothing goes live on argument alone. A frozen golden set of real cases — starting with every video you've judged — and any proposed change is replayed against it first: does the new hook rule actually pick better hooks on the 30 cases we know the truth about? Passing that, changes roll out staged: a few videos first, guardrails watched, auto-rollback if worse, widen only if better. This was GLM's Task 5 — absorbed here as a first-class organ, credit where due.

Have the raw material: judged videos with frames in the drop branch, the bake-off workflow pattern for A-vs-B harnesses.
Build the harness: golden case store + replay gate wired into the Proposal Desk + staged-rollout bookkeeping.
11

The Inspector

site + GitHubNew — from the pro comparison

Data validation before anything reaches pixels or the public: dates match sources (the JAN 1 2024 badge dies here), names match faces, images match the person being spoken about, links resolve, schema holds. Pros validate data before promotion for a reason — one wrong fact on a brand called receipts, not gossip costs more than a hundred good videos earn.

Have fragments: the gates, verify.php, the vision judge's mismatch check, the new story gate.
Build the pre-render contract: a checklist every feed item passes before the renderer accepts it; a failed parse renders nothing, never a wrong value.
12

The Playbook of Wins

site DBNew — from the pro comparison

Procedural memory — the third pillar the agent literature says v2 forgot. Rules say what to believe; skills say how to do it: the hook construction that held viewers, the caption formula that got clicks, the receipt-reveal rhythm that earned rewatches — stored as versioned, reusable templates the Doers compose, each carrying the record ids that proved it. Voyager's skill library, pointed at videos instead of Minecraft.

Have the ancestors: hook archetypes rotating in video_write_script(), the style variants, social_copy.php's per-platform formats — hard-coded skills nobody can version or retire.
Build the library: skills as data with win-rates, so a losing skill retires and a winning one spreads.
13

The Governor

GitHubSeeded — widen it

The watchdog grown up. Today it answers "is the brain alive?" The pro version also answers: "is performance drifting?" — views fell 60% week-over-week, retention slid 3 weeks running, a poster silently stopped — and "are we about to hit a wall?" — free-tier quotas near exhaustion, render minutes burning too fast. Alarms land in the room before you'd have noticed by looking.

Have ai-health (probes every model, red X + email), metrics_collector (the numbers exist).
Build the drift rules over the numbers already collected + quota tracking. Small, mostly SQL.

The two loops

Same engine, two speeds — marketing first

Loop B · distribution · runs first

Does it travel?

Cheap to test, fast signal, decides whether anyone sees anything at all.

  • caption · cover frame · title · hashtags · spoken search phrase
  • posting time · cadence · platform order
  • pinned comment · CTA wording · end-frame · replies
  • cross-post (Reddit, X, Bluesky) · bio · link order
  • the funnel: view → follow → profile → click → site
Loop A · craft · earns its cost

Is it good?

Expensive to test — every variant is a render. Learns from your verdicts and retention curves more than counts.

  • story choice (the Producer's DEMAND × QUALITY weights)
  • hook archetype · structure · length · ending
  • direction: faces, receipts, shot rhythm
  • voice · captions · sound density · style variant
What the machine may never learn away

The publish gate. The alleged/reportedly framing. CC0-only audio. The variation floors that keep videos from converging into templated mass-production. The dignity rules on tragedy. The permanent holdout. And it may never touch the code that measures it — measurement and optimisation stay in separate files with separate owners.

Target is retention + follows, never raw views — 73.8% of self-improving systems in the literature cheated a proxy metric; these locks are why this one won't.

Honest limits

Still true, still stated

  • You stay in the loop. Approval is the guardrail that keeps a learning machine from teaching itself to be worse. As categories prove safe, approval can loosen per category — never disappear.
  • Progress won't be a straight line. The Proving Ground and staged rollouts exist so wrong ideas die cheap and early instead of live and late.
  • It learns only what's measurable — plus what you tell it. Your verdicts are the one channel that carries taste; every one also becomes a test case forever.
  • Always-on lives on GitHub, not your PC. Your machine hosts the room — where you watch and rule. The 31 workflows are the day-and-night part, free, already running.
